First release of LRCSSync, a lyrics timing editor that saves `.lrc` files. Windows, macOS, Linux and Android builds are all below.

- Open a song and see its waveform and frequency view.
- Drag the voice band over the singer's frequencies. The app shows where singing starts and stops and snaps lines to it.
- Get lyrics from LRCLIB, a `.lrc` or `.txt` file, or paste them in.
- Time lines by tapping along to the song, dragging them on the timeline or nudging them in 10/50/100 ms steps.
- Press and hold on the timeline to zoom in while you hold.
- Half speed playback, loop a line, move all following lines together, undo and redo.
